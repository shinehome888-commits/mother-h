Prophetess Mother Hellen

Prophetess Mother Hellen, also known as Prophetess Hsagbata, is a woman of extraordinary faith and divine calling. Through years of fervent prayer, unwavering dedication, and the undeniable power of the Holy Spirit, she has built a ministry that touches lives across nations.

She is a well — a deep, flowing well of living water. From her life, rivers of healing, deliverance, and prophetic insight spring forth to quench the thirst of souls and renew the weary. Where she ministers, dry places bloom and broken lives are made whole.

Destiny Praise Chapel Healing and Deliverance Ministry International was established with a singular mission: to lead people to Christ through prayer, healing, deliverance, revival, worship, biblical teaching, and the transforming power of the Holy Spirit.

Under her anointed leadership, thousands have experienced miraculous healings, spiritual deliverance, and life transformation.
