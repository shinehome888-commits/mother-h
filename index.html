Prophetess Mother Hellen

Prophetess Mother Hellen, also known as Prophetess Hsagbata, is a woman of extraordinary faith and divine calling. Through years of fervent prayer, unwavering dedication, and the undeniable power of the Holy Spirit, she has built a ministry that touches lives across nations.

Destiny Praise Chapel Healing and Deliverance Ministry International was established with a singular mission: to lead people to Christ through prayer, healing, deliverance, revival, worship, biblical teaching, and the transforming power of the Holy Spirit.

Under her anointed leadership, thousands have experienced miraculous healings, spiritual deliverance, and life transformation. Her ministry extends beyond the walls of the church, reaching souls through crusades, revivals, online teachings, and personal prayer sessions.

Today's Devotion

📖

"For I know the plans I have for you," declares the LORD, "plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future."

— Jeremiah 29:11 (NIV)

Today, God reminds you that His plans for your life are perfect, purposeful, and filled with hope. No matter what season you are in, trust that the Author of your story is writing a beautiful chapter. Rest in His sovereignty and walk in the confidence that your future is secure in His hands.

🙏 Today's Prayer:
Heavenly Father, I thank You for Your perfect plans for my life. I choose to trust You in every season. Remove all fear, doubt, and anxiety from my heart.
